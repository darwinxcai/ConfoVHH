## Result and permissible interpretation

The frozen evidence-tier-first selector retained its original failure: seed2_model_4, DockQ 0.1762564824406321. Burial alone, predictor confidence, clash-fraction-v1 and overlap-burial-v1 all selected seed2_model_0, DockQ 0.48462880897372757, the best available candidate. All ten poses were scored in all five arms, with no ties or missing outcomes. Both new methods were declared before this calculation, after this case's outcomes were known. There were no additional variants or parameter searches.

The finite penalties avoid the specific absolute-zero preference on this exposed case. **They provide no selected-pose advantage over the two simple baselines here.** This result cannot establish which redesign, if any, should be preferred. No implementation bug was corrected, no production policy was changed, and no independent validation was performed. The old negative result and failed execution histories remain preserved without amendment.

The selectors used full modeled receptor A and VHH B, without experimental observed-region masks or native contacts. The engineered fusion was not removed. DockQ was joined from the preserved evaluation after ranking. Reference-free computation does not remove outcome-informed method-development bias: the analyst already knew this case's failure and best pose.

## Language for structural audit claims

Use “passes the specified geometric checks,” “no severe overlap under this measurement policy,” or “structural warning.” The historical word “supported” is retained verbatim in old audit records but does not mean demonstrated pose correctness. A geometry warning can coexist with a more accurate binding arrangement. Predictor confidence is also a prediction, not observed accuracy.

Do not claim that these formulas are force-field energies or MolProbity scores. Phenix describes clash assessment that adds hydrogens and reports severe atom overlaps per 1,000 atoms; our deliberately limited heuristics use heavy-atom residue contacts, the frozen 0.6 Å scale, and no added hydrogens. That literature motivates considering burden and severity; it does not validate these formulas or their ranking efficacy. [Phenix validation documentation](https://www.phenix-online.org/documentation/reference/validation.html).

## Next evidence required for a selection claim

The two exploratory formulas and code are versioned in the freeze manifest. Neither is chosen as a winner from this case. Before opening any new target's predictions or outcomes:

1. Finish and commit a complete prospective protocol and general input implementation. Specify eligibility, chain-role confirmation using prediction inputs, model/alternate-location handling, missing atoms, unusual residues, zero contacts, missing confidence, all failures, generator budget, seeds, and exact dependency and source hashes. The current extractor deliberately verifies only this frozen case; it is not a ready general evaluator. Verify a general implementation on synthetic structures and already exposed development data, then freeze it. No performance-guided edits on the held-out set.
2. Freeze the candidate target inventory, provenance and exposure register. Exclude 3P0G and every previously inspected development case from independent validation. Include prior repository development exposures, not merely cases discussed in this addendum. Do not select targets by whether the methods disagree or by outcome quality. Generator training/template exposure and method-development exposure are separate limitations to document.
3. Define independent groups before outcome access using receptor family/construct relationships and nanobody sequence/lineage relationships. Make a graph where any prespecified relatedness relation joins two cases; use connected components so a related receptor OR nanobody links cases transitively. Assign components wholly to development or holdout. Freeze sequences, annotations, alignment procedures, any numeric thresholds, and adjudication rules against the actual inventory. Uncertain relatedness cannot be treated as certified independence. The current record certifies zero new independent groups; it does not invent a population or threshold after viewing its outcomes.
4. Retain all five arms on identical candidate pools, including the original failing selector. Predeclare primary contrasts and multiplicity handling for the two new hypotheses against confidence and burial. Also report a uniform-random selection baseline and the best available pose as a retrospective ceiling, not a deployable method. Freeze exact ties without ID tie breaking; report uniform mean and range over selected ties. Preserve per-target and per-group losses, ties, abstentions, generation failures and missing evaluations. Never silently remove an unsuccessful stratum.
5. Use a frozen reference-mapping and DockQ evaluation protocol, independent of selector features; include mapping checks and the API/CLI crosscheck. State whether the estimand is conditional selection quality or end-to-end generation yield, and report both separately where relevant. Freeze success thresholds, paired differences, group weighting, missing-outcome bounds and inferential procedures before evaluation. Group-level inference must respect both receptor and nanobody relatedness; ten poses or repeated seeds are not independent units. Sample-size planning and required number of independent groups remain to be determined before registration, not after observing wins.
6. On new failures, retain the locked result. Any score, eligibility, grouping or mapping change becomes a separately versioned development exercise; those examined cases become exposed for the changed method. Report original and revised results side by side and require fresh independent cases for subsequent validation.

These are requirements for a future study, not a claim that that study is already registered or completed. No new target or GPU allocation is part of this work.

## Evidence required for a narrower structural-audit paper

A defensible audit claim still needs measured utility. Freeze specific user tasks such as confirming chain roles, finding a defined geometric issue, tracing a warning to atoms, and reconstructing provenance. Evaluate factual warning accuracy and provenance completeness against independently adjudicated examples; include negative controls and warning-free examples. Measure errors, time, and interpretation (especially whether readers mistake a warning tier for pose correctness) against a named existing workflow. Where users participate, plan a suitable study before recruitment. Do not infer utility from a screenshot, successful file parsing, or this selector comparison. No such utility study has been performed here.
