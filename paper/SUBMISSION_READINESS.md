# ConfoVHH software/application submission readiness

Updated 8 September 2026. **Not submission-ready; venue undecided.**

The near-term article describes an auditable model-review workflow and its
completed retrospective application. Its success criterion is a useful,
reusable, verifiable software contribution. It does not require presenting the
current ranking as superior, and it must not imply that the independent
hard-decoy protocol has passed. The formally cleared independent eligible-group
count remains **0** in the latest census record.

Missing original models need not be replaced with invented values or silently
removed from the denominator. A retained-subset application remains possible if
the available inputs are deposited, the omitted records are explicitly
accounted for, and claims remain limited to that subset. If full raw replay
cannot be provided, the submission must state exactly what can be reproduced
and the chosen venue must permit that availability arrangement.

## Scope of the workflow evaluation

Use a previously designated public development example or a clearly synthetic
software fixture with an instruction sheet and expected identity/provenance
fields. The task is to load the example, confirm roles, identify whether PAE is
available and mapped, inspect the documented evidence, and export a decision
record. Record completion and errors against the specified steps. This can
establish that another researcher can use and reproduce the workflow; it cannot
establish better binder selection, faster review, or fewer scientific mistakes
without an appropriately designed comparison.

Preparing the materials and failure criteria is executable repository work.
Obtaining a real participant's completion, authorship confirmation, and archive
redistribution permission requires the corresponding person's participation.
No invitation or external contact is authorized by this readiness record.

## Venue guidance checked on 8 September 2026

The [Bioinformatics Advances author guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformaticsadvances/pages/author-guidelines)
describe Application Notes for openly accessible software systems and their
use, and require a data availability statement. The official search-index
extract supported those narrow points; full-page retrieval was unavailable in
this review. Formatting and all remaining requirements must be rechecked from
the accessible publisher instructions when a venue is selected. This category
is a possible fit, not an assurance of novelty, acceptance, or present readiness.

The [JOSS submission requirements](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html)
include public-development-history and demonstrated-research-use screening,
software availability, and AI disclosure. Its
[review checklist](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/review_checklist.html)
also requests statement of need, related-software positioning, design rationale,
and research impact. This record does not claim ConfoVHH is eligible for JOSS.
The software draft is venue-neutral and does not replace the existing detailed
application manuscript.

The current JOSS instructions explicitly require more than six months of public
active development and screen for demonstrated research use. ConfoVHH should
not be submitted to JOSS now. This does not rule out a later JOSS submission;
the near-term venue decision remains the software/application route after its
data-availability and independent-use gates are met.
