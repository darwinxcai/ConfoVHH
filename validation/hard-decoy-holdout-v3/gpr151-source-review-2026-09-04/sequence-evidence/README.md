# GPR151 and NB6 sequence-only comparison

Both deposited receptor sequences (9W3K_4 and 9W3L_1) are identical, 419 aa. Against canonical human UniProt Q8TDV0, the recorded global alignment has no gaps and 33 differing residues. Four differences are S86L, L127W, S200A and S300A. The remaining 29 differences lie within positions 222–252. That complete 31-aa block exactly matches frozen development receptor 6VI4_1 positions 195–225. The longest identical contiguous segment extends one residue to 32 aa. The canonical UniProt record was retrieved twice with identical response bytes. No terminal extension or truncation is present in deposited sequence relative to canonical; this does not establish whether expression/purification tags were used experimentally.

Both deposited NB6 sequences (9W3K_5 and 9W3L_2) are identical, 127 aa. Against frozen development 6VI4_2, the numbered variable domains match at 118/121 positions, frameworks at 88/91 positions (96.70%), and CDR3 at 15/15 positions. CDR1 and CDR2 are also identical. All three differences are in IMGT framework 2: development-to-candidate V45A, S46P, N48K. This passes the existing frozen VHH sequence criterion. The Nb39 references 5C1M and 6B73 do not pass that criterion. The original pinned numbering and affine BLOSUM62 helpers were used, and all 18 frozen development profiles reproduced exactly.

The files provide sequence-match evidence only. An engineered KOR-segment interpretation is supported by the match but has not been confirmed from primary Methods in this packet. Binding mechanism, construct provenance, formal leakage edges, entry eligibility and independent-component counts remain unadjudicated. No publications, coordinates, contacts, structural images or labels were inspected in this task. Canonical topology annotations are sequence metadata, not observations of the deposited structure.

Receptor comparisons and all sequences/hashes are in gpr151-receptor-comparison.json. NB6 numbered maps and all 18 comparisons are in nb6-local-comparison.json.
