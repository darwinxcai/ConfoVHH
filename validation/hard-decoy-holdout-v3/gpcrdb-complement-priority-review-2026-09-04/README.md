# GPCRdb complement priority review, 4 September 2026

This review covers eight direct-looking records and three ambiguous antibody-sequence hits. It records six entry-level exclusions and leaves five entries pending. These decisions do not establish a completed census or a new count of independent components.

| Records | Evidence-supported assessment | Entry decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6KNM, 6O3C | Exact entries already in the development registry | Exclude from holdout |
| 8W1V | ADRB2 receptor accession and full Nb60 sequence match development 5JQH | Exclude from holdout; primary methods still pending |
| 8HN1, 8HNN, 8K2W | Nb6 is recruited through a transplanted KOR intracellular loop | Exclude engineered binding sites |
| 8T8M, 8TAO | Direct mGlu5/Nb43 records with receptor, VHH and publication matches to historical 8T7H | Pending component integration and remaining eligibility |
| 7Y89, 8J24 | Primary text and sequence support scFv16 format | Pending whole-entry reconciliation |
| 8J6L | Strong scFv16 sequence relationship; primary review incomplete | Pending primary text and receptor construct mapping |

The missing citation for 8HN1 is recovered from [Shi et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08405-0), whose deposition statement names that exact entry. The article establishes a KOR-loop graft absent from its frozen receptor source mapping. Its stated Nb6 purification tag also differs from the deposited sequence terminus; that difference remains unresolved. The graft evidence independently establishes construct unsuitability.

The [SCH546738 paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41594-023-01175-5) and [AMG487 paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41421-023-00617-0) identify CXCR3 as the target and KOR as the graft donor. The receptor entity's multiple UniProt accessions must not be treated as interchangeable target identities.

The ambiguous entries illustrate why sequence screening was needed. 7Y89 has mismatched descriptions on two transducer/antibody entities and an unresolved receptor-species conflict. The 8J24 description `scGV16` conflicts with its paper and identical sister-entry scFv sequences. The 8J6L label `SVF16` also matches sister-entry scFv sequences, but its extended receptor construct and complete primary methods remain unresolved. Candidate-format evidence and whole-entry disposition are recorded separately.

`source-reviews.json` includes source facts, article-content hashes where available, exact frozen raw-metadata hashes, complete polymer inventories, verified canonical sequence hashes and reproducible metadata connections. Source excerpts are paraphrased; full articles are not copied into this package. Existing frozen records remain unchanged. Native coordinates, structural images, holdout labels and prediction results were not inspected. Formal component certification and target freeze remain blocked.
