# GPCRdb inventory complement metadata capture

The frozen 2026-08-29 GPCRdb inventory contains 1,716 entries. Removing the 287-entry historical keyword intersection leaves exactly 1,429 entries. All 1,429 are requested afresh in 58 batches, each repeated twice.

Captured: 1426; missing in both repeats: 3; raw responses: 116. Metadata signals do not establish VHH identity, direct receptor binding, exclusions, or independent components. Entries lacking a VHH descriptor remain pending and require sequence and source review.

This captures the complement of one frozen inventory. It does not claim an up-to-date or exhaustive GPCR census, completion of every discovery route, or completion of C.2 scientific review. The broader census remains incomplete and the protocol remains DRAFT/BLOCKED.
