# ConfoVHH experimental feasibility brief

Status: **internal draft; no vendor contacted, order placed, target selected or experimental result obtained**.

This brief can be completed while the separate computational census proceeds. It does not promise that buying experiments will solve the shortage of independent benchmark components.

## Choose the question before the assay

ConfoVHH's proposed computational endpoint ranks alternative poses for a fixed target–VHH pair. A binding assay on that pair cannot identify which pose is correct. A known binder binding again establishes an assay control, not model-ranking validation.

| Intended claim | Evidence to commission | Key limit |
|---|---|---|
| A VHH binds the intended target in a defined preparation | Concentration-dependent binding with appropriate target, irrelevant-binder and background controls; orthogonal confirmation where feasible | Binding alone does not identify the interface or receptor conformation |
| One predicted interface is more consistent with experiment | Prespecified, discriminating interface tests with folding/expression controls; direct structural determination if feasible | Mutational effects may arise from folding or allostery; absence of an effect need not prove no contact |
| A VHH prefers one target state | Matched state-defined preparations/conditions, comparable accessible target levels and a prespecified binding contrast; independent evidence of the state manipulation | Ligand presence alone is not proof of a unique conformation; avidity and expression differences can mimic preference |
| Ranking improves prospective experimental selection | Blinded predictions and fixed baseline selections over independently chosen target/binder groups, including appropriate negatives, with a prespecified experimental endpoint and sample-size calculation | A single pair or many technical repeats cannot establish general selection performance |

Start with one explicitly named biological question and an assay feasibility package. Label an initial one-target experiment a pilot regardless of the number of wells, repeats or concentrations. Computational reference-pose agreement and wet-lab binding/state results receive separate datasets and conclusions.

## Information required for a quote that can be executed

Fill these fields before sending a scientific work order:

- **Material ownership and identity:** authorized target and VHH sequences, exact constructs, tags, mutations, linker composition, supplier/source and sequence identifiers. Identify any third-party material restrictions. No employer or collaborator material is presumed available.
- **Target context:** full-length versus domain construct; expression system; membrane, nanodisc, detergent or cell-surface presentation; intended oligomeric partners; ligand/state conditions; available quantity and stability evidence. The chosen preparation must match the biological question.
- **Binder context:** monomeric VHH versus multivalent format; purification and identity checks; concentration determination; aggregation assessment; labeling/immobilization plan. Fix format across comparative arms.
- **Controls:** an assay-compatible positive control where available; irrelevant VHH; no-target/background control; expression/folding or ligand-binding controls for target perturbations; controls for nonspecific binding and assay presentation. Specify what makes a run technically invalid before data exist.
- **Experimental unit:** independent target/binder group for generalization; independent biological preparation for within-system reproducibility; technical repeats for measurement error. Specify randomization, blinded sample codes, batch layout and handling of failed preparations.
- **Endpoint and analysis:** exact primary measurement and contrast; concentration range justified by pilot/assay capability; replicate rationale; fit model and identifiability checks; censoring, exclusion and failed-run rules. Request uncertainty and individual replicate data, not only a fitted affinity number or a selected plot.
- **Pose-discriminating tests, if relevant:** nominate competing poses and preselect perturbations/measurements that distinguish them, while specifying structural-integrity controls and a rule for inconclusive results. Freeze predictions and experimental contrasts before the vendor sees results.
- **Deliverables:** raw instrument files, full sample map, sequence/construct and preparation records, QC, all repeats and failures, processing scripts or settings, analysis report and transfer of remaining material where agreed.
- **Commercial scope:** feasibility stage, decision point, itemized costs, schedule, material consumption and fallback options. Further experiments follow the predefined scientific rationale, including negative findings.

## Pilot decision record

Record the selected target/VHH, question, material access, assay format, controls, primary endpoint, independent preparations, technical repeats, estimated cost and timetable. Leave unknown values explicitly unresolved; a vendor's quote is not evidence that a target preparation or assay will work.

Proceed to a broader experiment only if the pilot demonstrates usable material and a reproducible assay capable of distinguishing the planned alternatives. Report inconclusive material/assay feasibility separately from a biological negative. A successful feasibility pilot cannot change any existing ConfoVHH validation flag.
