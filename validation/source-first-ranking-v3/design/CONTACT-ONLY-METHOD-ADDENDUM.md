# Experimental contact recovery before fresh outcome evaluation

The prediction-only smoke checks exposed a capacity limitation: the unchanged full physical audit could not run on seven of twenty recovered prospective predictions. CASR and GRM5 exceeded its 12,000-atom execution limit; LGR4 exceeded its worst-case surface-area calculation limit. All these predictions passed input identity and geometry checks, and their original producer confidence remained available. Without a separate contact calculation, the proposed CDR contact policy would have no contact information for the two newly outcome-tested groups.

The experimental v3 adapter now derives the contact phase directly from the exact frozen interface source, stopping before confidence aggregation and solvent-accessible surface-area calculation. Source/dependency hashes and the extraction boundary are checked. The original parser, numbering, atom selection, inclusive 4.5-Angstrom contact loop, residue-pair counting and 5,000,000 candidate-pair / 50,000 residue-pair guards remain in force. All parsed atoms in the selected roles enter that unchanged phase; no extra atom trimming, coordinate changes or sequence changes occur. Existing hydrogen, occupancy and alternate-location parser rules still apply. The full audit's limits and the production implementation are unchanged.

The standalone report is used only for a valid prediction lacking a full audit. A present full audit remains authoritative, including an unavailable CDR fraction. A corrupted bound audit is rejected. The new report has its own method and coordinate bindings and is never presented as a full physical audit or assigned an invented full-audit digest. Missing standalone evidence preserves the frozen source-order fallback. Downstream calibration and outcome consumers independently recompute each standalone report from its bound coordinates and replay the saved ranks before reading labels.

Before any fresh prediction/native outcomes, the derived contact phase matched every available contact field in 1,058 full audits: all 995 historical poses, 50 new development smoke poses and 13 prospective smoke poses. All seven audit-limited smoke predictions produced contact evidence. Sixty focused tests passed; an independent review reproduced the seven recovered reports and their saved ranks. The complete test and implementation bindings are in `round3-ranking/CONTACT-PREPARATION-IMPLEMENTATION-FREEZE.json`, SHA256 `e7e8bb1e6a67e3079a04391970082047101e73e396eab43367ba930ffedf50bf`.

The same pre-outcome review hardened canonical-view reuse: the prescribed receptor ordering, selected nanobody and chain namespace must agree with the launch plan, and the canonical coordinates and complete identity map must reproduce exactly. All 70 actual smoke mappings were already correct and remained unchanged. This prevents accepting a self-consistent view of the wrong identical subunit.

This addendum changes experimental feature availability and identity validation only. It leaves the prespecified ranking formulas, source-gap grid and selection rule, target enrollment, generation settings, outcome metric, reporting strata and prohibition on production promotion unchanged. It does not establish improved ranking accuracy; the sealed development and prospective comparisons determine that separately.
