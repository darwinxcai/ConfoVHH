# Interpretation of the completed retrospective comparison

**Reproducibility is demonstrated for these preserved records.** Coordinate/confidence associations and originating predictor metadata were verified, the frozen geometry and original negative result were reproduced, and a separately frozen exploratory baseline recovered each predictor's exported ranking field. Rounded AlphaFold score ties were retained rather than presented as recovered internal tie-breaking. Five missing candidates remain explicit. These findings support computational traceability, not independent validation.

**Structural-audit utility remains untested as a practical claim.** The software reproducibly reports its defined geometric measurements and warnings. This does not establish that those warnings improve a user's decisions, save time, identify biologically relevant defects, or demonstrate pose correctness. Such utility requires a separately designed evaluation with appropriate ground truth or user/task outcomes.

**Predictive selection performance is mixed in this exposed four-complex corpus.** The native-confidence baseline completes the available descriptive comparison, while the five original methods and their abstentions remain unchanged. The frozen ConfoVHH failure on 3P0G persists; both graded methods lose to native confidence and burial on 4MQS, and outperform the native-score tie mean on 5JQH. No independent generalization or overall selection-superiority claim follows from these related, previously inspected, partly missing, mixed-predictor cases. Held-out selection performance, training novelty and operational audit utility remain unestablished.
