# Prospective benchmark v1: implementation and preparation freeze

**Execution blocked: zero certified independent groups.** This is a frozen evaluator, methods/analysis specification and retained-metadata inventory snapshot. It is not a completed target-set seal, completed prospective study, or permission to generate. The runnable prospective target and attempt sets are empty. A subsequent version must bind eligible targets and all target-specific inputs before execution; no result-driven implementation changes are permitted after that seal.

This protocol is separate from historical hard-decoy v3. It does not recover v3's distinct ranker, inherit its ten-group minimum as a power calculation, or rewrite its ledgers and gates. All five methods from PR #59 are preserved byte for byte. No additional variant or tuning on 3P0G is performed.

## Prediction-only evaluator contract

`scripts/benchmark/evaluator.py score` accepts a strict JSON plan and original coordinate/confidence artifacts. The plan has studyId, targets and attempts. Each target declares id, groupId, receptorChain, vhhChain, receptorSequence and vhhSequence from prediction inputs. Each attempt declares id, targetId, producer status (`generated`, `failed`, `not-run`), reason, and coordinate/confidence file bindings (path, byte size, SHA-256; missing confidence is null). Additional keys, including native masks, DockQ, supplied geometry features, reference coordinates or manually supplied confidence values, are rejected. Groups affect analysis weighting, not scientific rank keys.

Supported population: arbitrary target sequences in the canonical 20-amino-acid alphabet, with separate receptor and VHH protein chains and pinned Boltz mmCIF outputs. Chains are explicitly identified; no native alignment or name-based role guess is used by the selector. Exactly coordinate model 1, full sequence-indexed CA coverage, agreeing author/label chain and residue IDs, no insertion codes or alternate locations, occupancy 1, no duplicate heavy atoms, finite positions, and at most 12,000 selected heavy atoms are required. These are input-format and identity restrictions, not accuracy thresholds. Missing non-CA backbone or sidechain atoms remain visible through the frozen audit rather than being repaired. Multi-model files, partial sequence coverage, noncanonical polymers and unsupported elements are retained as evaluation failures. No relaxation, coordinate repair, residue cropping, fusion masking, chemical optimization, or replacement generation occurs.

All modeled residues in the declared roles contribute, including engineered receptor regions. Other complex chains/ligands can remain in the source file but do not enter A/B geometry. Original whole-complex Boltz `confidence_score` is the confidence comparator, not an interface-specific estimate. Invalid/missing confidence has a distinct reason and is never replaced with pLDDT, ipTM or another field. Hashes bind declared files; they do not authenticate an upstream generator or establish biological role truth. The target-input provenance seal remains a prerequisite for prospective use.

The pinned, packaged v0.6 engine computes actual canonical-frame half-delta-SASA, contact/clash measurements and the historical warning tier for each coordinate file; no caller-provided burial or warning level is accepted. Its 41 source/dependency identities are checked before execution. An independent decimal-coordinate calculation verifies residue and atom contacts and severe clash counts, and calculates overlap burden. The scoring environment is Node 24.19.0, Python 3.12.14, NumPy 1.26.4 and Biopython 1.88. DockQ 2.1.3 is used only by the separate reference stage. The existing full analysis dependency lock is included by hash in the preparation freeze.

## Five unchanged methods

Let B be exact frozen half-delta-SASA, N the number of residue-pair contacts at <=4.5 Å, C the number of those pairs containing a nonexempt overlap >=0.6 Å, and Q the mean of `(positiveMaximumPairOverlap/0.6)^2` over all N pairs. Frozen radii and the CYS SG–SG exemption remain unchanged. There is no native-derived region definition.

1. **frozen-v06:** descending `(evidenceTier, B)`.
2. **burial-only:** descending B.
3. **predictor-confidence:** descending original confidence_score.
4. **clash-fraction-v1:** descending `B*(1-C/N)`.
5. **overlap-burial-v1:** descending `B/(1+Q)`.

Keys and exact floating-point ties are unchanged. IDs only label rows. Full tied sets are retained; no alphabetical or confidence tie break. Historical “supported” means a specified audit tier, never demonstrated pose correctness. The graded formulas are still unvalidated hypotheses, not physical energies.

## Selection sets, missing data and failures

One target's two seeds and five models per seed form one selection set of ten planned attempts. All five arms receive the same set of evaluable generated coordinates. Failed/not-run attempts and malformed/mismatched coordinates stay in the attempt ledger and do not generate replacements. This conditional pool is reported alongside all planned attempts and generation/evaluation yield; its results are not end-to-end generation success.

If any candidate in that pool lacks a required feature for an arm, that entire arm abstains on that target, preserving partial rank rows for diagnosis. In particular N=0 makes the existing graded formulas undefined; it is not silently assigned a score of zero or omitted. A missing confidence score withholds confidence selection for the entire pool. Targets with no evaluable coordinates remain explicit abstentions in every arm. The receipt distinguishes complete scoring from failures/abstentions; process exit alone is not a scientific PASS.

After all score files are saved and hash-bound, a separate `dockq-outcomes.py` process reads a separately bound reference manifest. It uses fixed receptor/VHH chain-role mappings, the first reference model loaded by pinned DockQ, default sequence alignment (`no_align=False`), and no chain-map search or outcome-maximizing remapping. The reference manifest must identify single-character chain IDs for the API/CLI contract and exact reference bytes; native/reference role and assembly identity must be certified before use. Default sequence alignment can be ambiguous at repeated tags or missing regions; such known ambiguities require a prespecified adjudication, not a retrospectively chosen better mapping. No reference is exposed to `score`.

The first evaluable candidate of each target receives an independent DockQ CLI/API crosscheck with an absolute tolerance of 1e-12. Commands have 120-second limits with raw stdout/stderr preserved. A reference-evaluation failure stops further evaluation, preserves the failed record, and marks remaining outcomes missing pending diagnosis. Missing references, null native interfaces and failed crosschecks never become zero-valued DockQ. There is no automatic mapping repair or timeout extension. All ten attempt dispositions must be represented in the outcome join, including failed generation.

For a tie, selected DockQ is the uniform mean across all selected candidates, with range. If any selected outcome is missing, the complete mean is unavailable and bounds assign missing values 0 and 1; abstention bounds are [0,1], not an asserted accuracy. The uniform-random pool baseline is computed without drawing random candidates. Best available is a retrospective ceiling with all ties; it is marked incomplete if any evaluable candidate lacks DockQ. Both are descriptive baselines, not additional scoring variants.

## Primary comparisons and analysis

Four primary contrasts are retained: each of the two graded hypotheses versus predictor confidence and versus burial alone. No hypothesis is chosen as primary because of its 3P0G behavior. All five methods and all losses are reported.

The target-level endpoint is expected selected DockQ under uniform scientific ties. Compute paired differences within target, average targets equally within each declared relatedness group, and average groups equally. Extra seeds, poses, constructs and repeated target records do not increase the independent sample count. Group membership cannot change after outcomes are inspected.

Report every target and group difference, wins, exact zero ties and losses. The confirmatory test addresses a group's probability of a strictly positive paired difference, not a positive population mean: a one-sided exact binomial test against 0.5, with ties counted as non-wins. Holm correction controls the family of four comparisons at 0.05. Report the mean group difference descriptively alongside this test; a win-rate test is not evidence that the mean effect is positive. The code does not automatically claim superiority. Independence and representative sampling remain study assumptions requiring external certification.

If any planned group's contrast is unavailable, primary aggregate estimates and tests for that contrast are withheld rather than dropping the group. Worst/best bounds still propagate across all planned groups. No complete-case significance rescue or alternative endpoint is substituted. Generation/evaluation failure counts, selection abstention rates, random-pool baseline and retrospective ceiling are mandatory descriptive outputs. Any exploratory endpoint later added must be versioned and separated from these primary comparisons.

Feasibility is based on **G=0 actually certified groups**, not the former target minimum or provisional component counts. No power estimate is fitted from 3P0G. The stored sensitivity calculation shows explicit assumed group-win probabilities and required independent-group counts for 80% per-comparison power using alpha/4 as a conservative Holm planning bound. Those are required sample sizes under assumptions, not discovered targets. Even seven all-winning independent groups is only a mathematical testing floor, not adequate planned power.

## Eligibility and relatedness

The finite discovery ledger enumerates the retained search-union IDs plus explicit prior controls. It is a historical metadata frame, not an exhaustive current search or a claim that every ID is a GPCR/VHH target. Every unreviewed ID remains unreviewed. No source availability, reference score or performance result is used to select favorable targets.

Before a target can enter the prospective seal, require: source-backed direct binding of a separate VHH chain to the receptor; exact prediction-available construct and chemical identities; a deposited reference with resolution <=4.0 Å and a certifiable receptor/VHH assembly and mapping; documented prior-exposure disposition; complete receptor/nanobody ancestry and relatedness review; compatible full-sequence inputs; fixed local MSA artifacts; and adequate resources for the fixed generator settings. Do not infer atom-level reference quality solely from global resolution. Unknown role, construct, ancestry or exposure remains blocked, not a pass. Exposed development cases, parser controls, known auxiliary binders, receptor/VHH same-chain fusions and known development-connected targets are excluded from independent validation.

Retain the existing receptor TM1–TM7 primary sequence rule (>=40% global identity and >=80% coverage on each sequence) and IMGT nanobody rule (framework >=90%, CDR3 >=70%, absolute CDR3 length difference <=2), with the already pinned alignment/numbering contracts. Known parent/variant relationships and same receptor/source-family links are additional vetoes. The retained GPCRdb first-three-level family prefix is a conservative grouping rule. Connect cases when either receptor OR VHH relatedness applies and take transitive connected components. A provisional VHH sequence connection with unresolved binding role blocks clearance but is not described as a proved biological lineage. Absence of a threshold match does not certify unrelatedness; missing profiles, unreviewed ancestry, fusion grafts and uncertain source relationships remain blocked. The grouping output explicitly distinguishes provisional components from certified independent groups.

The inventory retains 17 original native-development entries plus two additional legacy-development references, four prior parser controls, and recorded structural/prediction-prose exposure events. Exposure families remain blocked even if no coordinates were previously opened. Predictor training/template overlap is assessed separately from method-development exposure; a future method-development-held-out test must not claim training novelty without the relevant predictor-training evidence.

## Freeze boundary and honest alternative

The preparation manifest freezes code, source/dependency locks, policy, inventory and group snapshot, analysis plan, tests and feasibility record. **The complete runnable prospective study remains unsealed**, because no group meets all requirements and target-dependent artifacts do not exist. New eligibility/source evidence can be added in a new preparation version without opening outcomes; any implementation change after outcome access invalidates held-out status for the affected development exercise.

An available alternative is an explicitly **retrospective, exposure-aware development and structural-audit evaluation**. Keep the verified ten-pose 3P0G comparison unchanged and recover/checksum-verify compatible already-exposed legacy coordinate files for evaluator coverage, provenance and warning correctness. The existing recovery inventory lists 355 coordinate records as present-but-unverified and 105 missing across six reference IDs; these are not 355 independent cases, and their receptor/VHH roles and compatibility must be checked. Do not report legacy DockQ-like numbers as newly verified DockQ. A prospective audit-utility study can instead measure task errors, provenance traceability and warning interpretation on predeclared examples; it needs its own ground truth and participant/task plan. Neither alternative validates a superior pose selector. No alternative evaluation or generation is run by this preparation task.
