Status: implementation specification approved by the parent before fitting. Fitting awaits the qualified biological-group map and an explicit fit release. All round 3 outcomes are development-exposed. No reserved-receptor outcomes are permitted.

## Fixed comparison and scope

1. Original saved producer confidence, preserving its exact scalar and direction.
2. Centered ridge using four prediction-confidence features: `complex_plddt`, `iptm`, `complex_iplddt`, and `log1p(complex_ipde)`.
3. The same model with three additional prediction-only contact features: `log1p(contactPairCount)`, CDR-contact-pair share of all contact pairs, and unnumbered-contact-pair share of all contact pairs.

The framework share is determined by the other two shares and is not another fitted input. The original confidence scalar is approximately 0.8 × complex pLDDT + 0.2 × ipTM, so it is not duplicated as a learned input; the baseline always uses the original saved scalar. No native-reference quantity, outcome, target name, sequence, receptor identity, seed, generation-arm identifier, group identifier, or reference resolution is an inference feature. Full PAE/PDE arrays are unnecessary for this prespecified comparison. Boltz model weights and inference are unchanged by ranker training.

The primary learned scope is the pinned Boltz-2 pair-confidence context. The 50 dimer and 25 helix-context predictions retain source ranking and remain in all-planned reporting, explicitly identified as fallback-only cases. Two dimer groups and one helix group cannot support a useful independent nested training comparison. No cross-context transfer is claimed.

The initial 300 rows have all seven features, all inputs valid, and contacts/numbering available. The nine pair-context sets comprise eight groups before the new lineage qualification. These counts may merge under the independent relatedness audit; neither folds nor inference infer groups from target names. The new 900 development runs repeat these same twelve cases and do not add independent biological groups.

Producer support also binds a versioned normalized generation regime. It contains recycling/sampling steps, sample count, step scale, precision, templates/restraints/potentials, effective MSA maximum/subsampling settings and kernel choice. Seeds, output-only flags, file format, worker count, timeout and MSA retrieval transport do not create different scientific regimes. The incoming baseline reuses the same captured alignment bytes and therefore has the same normalized baseline regime; the two changed generation arms have distinct explicit profiles. Group-count minima use complete learner-eligible pools for that model family, not every pair-context label.

## Fitting and weighting

Each comparison pool is one target × generation arm × prespecified seed batch. Never combine candidates from arms for the primary analysis. All pools/constructs/seeds in one qualified receptor/Nb lineage group stay together in both outer and inner folds. Weights are equal across biological groups, equal across target sets within a group, equal across generation arms within a target, equal across seed batches within an arm, then equal across rows. This prevents extra seeds or generation arms from creating extra independent weight.

For each training pool, subtract the feature mean and DockQ mean. Compute each feature's weighted root-mean-square centered value from the training pools only. A zero scale becomes one. Divide by these saved training scales; no validation/test pool contributes to learned normalization. Solve weighted squared error plus `lambda * sum(beta**2)` with no intercept. This is a small linear within-pool ranker, not an absolute DockQ or binding-probability estimator. Ranking uses raw feature values divided by the saved scales; subtracting a common pool mean cannot affect its ordering.

The fixed lambda grid is `[0.01, 0.1, 1, 10]`. Unchanged source is an explicit selection candidate. Inner leave-one-biological-group-out validation chooses the highest equal-group expected first-choice DockQ, provided no inner held-out pool has lower acceptable-first probability than source. Exact objective ties prefer source, then the larger lambda. If every fitted candidate violates that guard, choose source. Every outer held-out group is evaluated using only the inner choice and model fit on the other groups. At least three supported groups are required for this nested procedure; insufficient groups produce explicit source fallback. The final model uses the same inner procedure on all authorized development groups. Results from that final full-data fit are apparent development results, never the nested estimate.

## Missingness and ties

All planned rows stay in the attempt ledger. Failed or not-run generation attempts are not candidate structures: they stay in generation-availability counts but do not poison source ranking among produced candidates. Original source ranking abstains when any produced candidate lacks finite source confidence, or when no candidate was produced. Invalid produced coordinates do not erase a row or its source score. A learned arm falls back for the entire produced pool if any candidate has invalid input, a required optional feature is missing, or the version/model/context/regime is outside its frozen support. It does not mix learned and source score scales within a pool. Valid no-contact structures are not input corruption: count is zero, contact shares are unavailable, and the interface model falls back. The confidence-only model can still operate when all its features and validity checks exist. No partial training pool is fitted: incomplete feature pools are explicitly excluded from that model's fitting but retained as source fallbacks in every validation metric. Missing/unavailable outcomes for produced candidates prevent fitting or complete evaluation; failed attempts need no reference outcome. Generation absence and reference-outcome absence are reported separately.

Equal scalar scores are scientific ties. IDs only stabilize serialization. First and top-k summaries uniformly average over the relevant tied boundary. A tie's acceptable-first probability is its acceptable fraction; the loss guard compares that probability, not an arbitrary ID-selected pose.

## Reporting and exploratory gate

Report nested held-out first-choice DockQ as the primary development estimate, all-planned and learner-supported scopes separately; first-choice acceptable probability, lost/rescued acceptable choices, top-five mean quality/count, coverage/fallback/abstention counts, and available best-candidate gap accompany it. Report every outer group and every selected inner setting. Do not use random pose splits or treat seeds/arms as independent replication. Candidate-generation comparisons use identical counts and remain separate from ranking comparisons.

A candidate can proceed toward a frozen reserved evaluation only if its nested learner-supported equal-group first-choice gain is at least **0.02 DockQ**, with no acceptable-first probability loss in any outer held-out pool and unchanged selection coverage. This is an exploratory useful-gain gate, not statistical significance or proof of generalization. The contact features have prior negative ranking results; their inclusion is a fresh small comparison, not an assumed improvement. A model that fails the gate is retained as a negative result; source remains the candidate baseline.

Confidence-only and confidence-plus-interface are two separately prespecified model families. Their outer estimates evaluate each family's fitting/selection procedure separately. Choosing a family after inspecting those two outer estimates is exploratory model selection; the chosen family's outer estimate is not an unbiased estimate of that additional family-selection step. A later reserved evaluation tests the single frozen choice.

## Artifact separation

Prediction extraction authenticates saved v3 files, source adapters, raw confidence files, generation receipts, coordinate identities and producer profiles. It emits no outcome field. Outcome authentication is a separate explicitly authorized step over development receipts. Grouping is a separate qualified map. Training requires a release binding this protocol, code, prediction table, labels, grouping, exact allowed IDs and allowed development roles. Saved models bind feature names, transformations, scales, coefficients, support profiles, lambda, training membership, input hashes and fold assignments. Inference reads a model and prediction rows only; outcome injection is rejected. No old scientific artifact is overwritten.
